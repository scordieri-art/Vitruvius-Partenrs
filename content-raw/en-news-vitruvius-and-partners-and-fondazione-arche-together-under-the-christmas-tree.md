### VITRUVIUS & PARTNERS AND FONDAZIONE ARCHÉ: TOGETHER UNDER THE CHRISTMAS TREE

<!-- post-info --> December 27, 2023 · 8:00 am · News

![](https://vitruviusandpartners.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/12/VitruviusPartners-e-Fondazione-Arche-1024x683.jpg)  
<!-- immagine: immagine in evidenza del post -->

Arché is a non-profit foundation that supports vulnerable children and families in building social, housing, and employment autonomy by providing support and care services. It operates in Milan, Rome, and San Benedetto del Tronto.

This year, for Christmas, we decided to share a part of this very important journey by delivering a load of gifts under the tree.

Illustrated books, tickets for dance performances and basketball games, creative activity tools, sports equipment… Arché provided us with a detailed list of the children’s wishes. And we granted them. A simple gesture that, hopefully, has brought many smiles.

Happy holidays to everyone from the Vitruvius team!

[Arché foundation](https://arche.it/)
