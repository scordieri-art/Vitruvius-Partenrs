### SOA ATTESTATION: VITRUVIUS & PARTNERS ACHIEVES CERTIFICATION FOR CATEGORY OG1 – CALSS III

<!-- post-info --> February 24, 2023 · 2:29 pm · News

![](https://vitruviusandpartners.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/02/news-soa2-1024x683.jpg)  
<!-- immagine: immagine in evidenza del post -->

### Vitruvius & Partners, by obtaining this certification, has proceeded to comply with the new legislation intreoduced by Decree Law 21/2022, being able to continue to be entrusted with the execution of works covered by building bonuses.

Specifically, Decree-Law 21/2022 introduced, for the purposes of the recognition of the tax incentives set forth in Articles 119 and 121 of Decree-Law 34 of 19 May 2020, converted by Law 77 of July 2020, the obligation to entrust the execution of works – with an amount exceeding Euro 516.000,00 – to companies in possession, at the time of the signing of the relevant Contract, of the SOA certification.

The achievement of the aforementioned certificate, in addition to demonstrating the company’s full compliance with the continuously updated sector regulations, is a new milestone for Vitruvius & Partners, which will be able to participate in tenders for the execution of public works and wide-ranging projects, as well as being able to continue operating within the scope of building bonuses, guaranteeing clients the possibility of benefiting from the aforementioned tax incentives.

Vitruvius & Partners is proud to have achieved this important result, which was achieved thanks to the commitment of the entire company team.
