### Vitruvius and Partners for ABIO Association

<!-- post-info --> July 17, 2022 · 1:10 pm · V&P News

![](https://vitruviusandpartners.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/07/VitruviusTeam-1-1024x768.png)  
<!-- immagine: immagine in evidenza del post -->

It was a pleasure to cooperate with ABIO Association by making a donation to celebrate Easter holidays.

ABIO operates in pediatric wards with more than 5.000 volunteers, animating the day of children hospitalized and assisting their families during the hard time of hospitalisation. In return for our support, the Association sent us Easter eggs, which the company decided to give to all employees and collaborators.

Thank you ABIO for your amazing work and to all Vitruvius team!
