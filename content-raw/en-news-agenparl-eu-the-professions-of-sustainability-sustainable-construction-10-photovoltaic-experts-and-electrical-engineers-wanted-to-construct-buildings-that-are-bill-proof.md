### Agenparl.eu: The professions of sustainability – Sustainable construction, 10 photovoltaic experts and electrical engineers wanted to construct buildings that are bill-proof

<!-- post-info --> December 27, 2022 · 8:57 am · News

![](https://vitruviusandpartners.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/12/fotovoltaico_periti-1024x683.jpg)  
<!-- immagine: immagine in evidenza del post -->

### **This is the appeal made by Vitruvius & Partners, a general contractor leader in sustainable building, led by Vittorio Ligresti, Roberto Schiavo and Luca Limone, based in Milan but operating throughout the Italian territory, with a focus on Lombardy.**

Sustainable building is the frontier of new generation construction, but also of buildings that need to be renovated. A necessity given both by the new EU directive, which set the 2030 limit for new zero-emission buildings and 2050 for those already existing, and from the need to reduce costs and consumption to have buildings that are “bill proof”.
