### **Energy transition is the essential tool to achieve the European objective of full decarbonisation by 2050**

The energy transition consists in the transition from a system based entirely on electricity produced from fossil fuels to a new and more sustainable low-carbon system, powered by renewable sources.

The great importance of this transition is in the fundamental role that this can have in the fight against climate change. Renewable energy, actually, reduce pollution, while coal-fired power stations can be reconverted in a circular economy perspective.

Vitruvius and Partners is a leading company in the energy transition sector, thanks to the employment of highly specialized professionals and to the implementation of redevelopment and energy efficiency interventions, such as solar panel and energy storage systems installation or the realization of thermal coats.

Vitruvius & Partners actively contributes to green change and to sustainable development, through several projects in Italy – to enhance and redevelop the territory – but also looks to Europe with the intention of expanding its professional and ethical contribution towards energy transition beyond national boundaries.
