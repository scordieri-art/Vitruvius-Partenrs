### TECHNICAL TRAINING AND NEW SKILLS: HUMAN CAPITAL IN CONSTRUCTION 4.0

<!-- post-info --> July 15, 2025 · 8:18 am · News

![](https://vitruviusandpartners.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/Ai.png)  
<!-- immagine: immagine in evidenza del post -->

### Vitruvius & Partners invests in professional development to drive innovation and sustainability in the construction sector.

The construction industry is undergoing a significant transformation, driven by digitalization, sustainability, and the adoption of advanced technologies. To meet these challenges, it is essential to invest in human capital, equipping professionals with the skills needed to operate effectively in the environment of Construction 4.0.

Vitruvius & Partners, a company specializing in construction, renovations, and building services with operations nationwide, recognizes the importance of continuous training. **The company promotes the adoption of digital tools such as Building Information Modeling (BIM) and artificial intelligence to optimize design and site management**. These technologies require specific skills, which Vitruvius & Partners develops through internal training programs and collaborations with specialized institutions.

In Italy, initiatives like the 4.0 Skills School at the MADE Competence Center offer training paths to acquire expertise in Industry 4.0, including courses on augmented and mixed reality applied to construction sites. Events such as the Construction Innovation Days 2025, organized by TeamSystem, provide further opportunities for updates on digitalization, sustainability, and business management in the AEC sector.

**Vitruvius & Partners** actively participates in these initiatives, integrating the acquired skills into its business processes. The company also places great value on soft skills, such as leadership and team management, which are crucial for tackling the challenges of modern construction.
