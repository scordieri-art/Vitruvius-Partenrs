### Sustainable solutions for the energy transition: ground-mounted photovoltaics enhances the land without consuming valuable resources.

Ground-mounted PV is one of the most widespread and proven solutions for renewable energy production. By installing solar panels on flat surfaces, this technology maximizes solar irradiation, offering a clean alternative to fossil fuels.

One of the main advantages is the ability to install systems on vast, non-productive areas such as marginal land or decommissioned industrial sites, thus avoiding the use of high-value agricultural land. Additionally, the modular nature of these systems allows for customized installations, ranging from small plots to large solar parks capable of powering entire communities.

**Ground-mounted PV plants are designed to maximize efficiency: the optimal orientation and tilt of the panels ensure consistent energy production throughout the year. Moreover, new technologies, such as bifacial panels, allow sunlight to be captured from both the front and the rear, further increasing performance.**

In Italy, PV has made a significant contribution to the energy transition. Regions like Puglia and Sicily, characterized by high solar irradiation, host numerous plants that generate energy for thousands of families, reducing CO2 emissions and energy costs.
