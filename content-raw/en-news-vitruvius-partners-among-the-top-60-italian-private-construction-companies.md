### VITRUVIUS & PARTNERS AMONG THE TOP 60 ITALIAN PRIVATE CONSTRUCTION COMPANIES

<!-- post-info --> June 1, 2023 · 1:19 pm · News

![](https://vitruviusandpartners.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/06/top-60-1024x683.jpg)  
<!-- immagine: immagine in evidenza del post -->

### Vitruvius & Partners ranks 44nd in the list of the top 60 private construction companies in Italy.

This recognition acknowledges our hard work and unwavering dedication to our projects, clients, and employees.

The ranking, as updated on the 23th of May 2023, is the result of a study by the Milan-based [research company Guamari](https://www.guamari.it/). The study includes a thoroughly detailed financial report of the top 60 Italian private construction companies with a 2021 revenue over € 21.9 million.

Our company has therefore proved to stand out for the quality of its services, the highly skilled staff, and the constant pursuit of sustainable solutions to meet the clients’ needs.

Moreover, the revenue for 2022 has significantly exceeded the reported amount, reaching 73 million euros, and thus marking an impressive growth rate of 109%.

Given the strategic importance of the sector for the country’s economy, being among the big players spurs us to keep working with even greater commitment and passion: to enhance our offer, to further strengthen our position in the private construction industry and – first and foremost – to create new projects. With you and for you.
