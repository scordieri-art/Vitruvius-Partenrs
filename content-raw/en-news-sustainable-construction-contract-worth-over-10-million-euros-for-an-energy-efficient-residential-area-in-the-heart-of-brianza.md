### SUSTAINABLE CONSTRUCTION. CONTRACT WORTH OVER 10 MILLION EUROS FOR AN ENERGY-EFFICIENT RESIDENTIAL AREA IN THE HEART OF BRIANZA

<!-- post-info --> May 12, 2023 · 10:23 am · News

![](https://vitruviusandpartners.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/05/immagine-2-progetto-Civitas5-Airuno-VitruviusPartners2-1024x683.jpg)  
<!-- immagine: immagine in evidenza del post -->

### Vitruvius & Partners, in partnership with Panzeri Architetti, has launched a building requalification project focused on sustainability to give a new look to 58 apartments and reduce the entire residential estate’s electricity consumption by 50%.

The construction site is located in Airuno, in the province of Lecco, just a few steps away from the Lake of Como, and less than an hour from Milan, in the typical suburban landscape of Brianza. The project – a **contract worth over 10 million euros** – involves the complete eco-sustainable renovation of CIVITAS 5, a residential compound of 3 buildings with 58 apartments.

#### The project: green renovation

A comprehensive green approach will be taken to rethink the buildings. The renovation of the interior spaces will involve the use of **stone and wood**, while the external facades will feature **natural materials** along with lush **vegetation**, inspired by Boeri’s Bosco Verticale (a well-known reforestation project in the center of Milan). To ensure **energy efficiency**, doors and windows providing thermal and acoustic insulation, photovoltaic panels, wind power systems with storage units, and electric vehicle charging stations will be installed.
Designed and coordinated by Vitruvius & Partners in collaboration with architect Fabio Panzeri and his team of urban regeneration experts, the project will employ skilled workers and industry professionals.

#### Requalification means value

This combination of interventions will result in a **drastic reduction in energy consumption**: over the half of the current level (the residential estate has indeed been nicknamed “the bill-proof compound” already). At the same time, this will significantly **reduce** **CO2 emissions** and give a real contribution to improve the quality of the environment. Moreover, the constant focus on **style** – from project design to material supply selection – will provide buildings that are starting to show their signs of aging with a fresh look.
Because in Vitruvius & Partners’ vision, **requalification** **means value**: in terms of environmental, economic and aesthetic benefit. For the sake of the planet and of its inhabitants.
