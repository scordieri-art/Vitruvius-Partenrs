### Between the unemployed and discouraged, in Italy five million people to be included. The III edition of “I sentieri delle professioni” begins. Thousands of opportunities in December

Guidance is becoming increasingly important. Not just for young people looking for a future. **Between the unemployes and discouraged, indeed, there are almomst five million people to be included in the job market**, which becomes less and less efficient for a problem of mismatch between supply and demand. The paradox is that when unemployment arises the difficulty to find professional figures needed by companies doesn’t decrease. The new **Randstad Research** survey reveals this.
