### ISO 9001 CERTIFICATION: VITRUVIUS & PARTNERS ATTESTING COMPLIANCE WITH THE REGULATORY REQUIREMENTS FOR QUALITY MANAGEMENT SYSTEMS

<!-- post-info --> January 4, 2023 · 1:50 pm · News

![](https://vitruviusandpartners.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/01/Iso_9001-1024x576.jpg)  
<!-- immagine: immagine in evidenza del post -->

![](https://vitruviusandpartners.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/01/VP_ISO_LOGO-1024x218.png)

### Vitruvius & Partners, Contractor operating throughout the Italian territory, has recently obtained the ISO 9001 Certification in relation to services related to the maintenance of civil buildings.

Through this Certification, Vitruvius & Partners ensures the maintenance and improvement over time of the quality of its goods and services, thereby guaranteeing, increasing professionalism towards its customers and suppliers in terms of reliability and safety.
Obtaining such a Certification which is the internationally recognized reference standard for the company quality management, represents, then, an important objective pursued by Vitruvius & Partners with a view to constant growth and continuous improvement.
