### First home purchase, despite the general context the trend resists and the builders already look to the house of the future.

The propensity of Italians to invest savings for the purchase of the first house has never ceased from the post-war period to today. Instead the need for efficiency and savings on domestic consumption change. The last months have certainly not been the most favorable to engage in a large investment. The house, however, is home to family security not only economic but after the pandemic also that of the health.
