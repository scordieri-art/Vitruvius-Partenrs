### SOWING AND REAPING: END-OF-THE YEAR RECORDS AND THOUGHTS

<!-- post-info --> December 29, 2023 · 8:01 am · News

![](https://vitruviusandpartners.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/12/chi-semina-raccoglie-1024x682.jpg)  
<!-- immagine: immagine in evidenza del post -->

End of the year: time for assessments, reviews, reflections on the time spent, and resolutions for the upcoming one.

The activities of 2023 demanded great commitment on many fronts from everyone involved, yielding more than we could have imagined. As always happens when working together.

We have entered the ranking of the most important private construction companies in Italy (**[the top 65 Guamari](https://www.guamari.it/_files/ugd/30ba77_d36a1e701b7e4b9482efebdfd0e7c38b.pdf)**), securing the 24th position. We have obtained crucial funding to further enhance innovative solutions for sustainability and energy efficiency. We are developing requalification and real estate projects in strategic locations of great value for urban as well as suburban areas, and for the quality of life of their inhabitants. And we are about to close our financial records with a growth rate of 109%.

We feel satisfied, grateful to all the hands and minds that made these achievements possible, and ready to keep going with the same momentum.

But first, a toast to the ending year and a wish of profound gratitude to those who have been part of the journey, and will continue to be, towards new challenges and successes to share.
