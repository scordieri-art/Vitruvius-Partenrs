### ELECTRIC CAR CHARGING STATIONS: WHY INVEST IN THE ‘GREEN’ TURNAROUND

<!-- post-info --> March 9, 2023 · 2:18 pm · News

![](https://vitruviusandpartners.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/03/News_Colonnine-1024x683.jpg)  
<!-- immagine: immagine in evidenza del post -->

### From Brussels the stop to petrol and diesel cars from 2035, renewable energies will also be used in the automotive sector. There is an obligation for manufacturers to cut the production of endothermic vehicles in order to achieve a totally zero-emission fleet in 2050.

Vitruvius & Partners is positioning itself at the forefront of green change by contributing, through energy refurbishment services, to zero CO2 emissions into the environment. In the field of energy renovation, Vitruvius & Partners is an industry leader in particular in the installation of electric charging stations – along with other energy efficiency measures.

This green turn represents a huge opportunity for the creation and specialisation of a circular supply chain in the sector. But investing in the installation of electric recharging stations has numerous advantages for companies: in addition to contributing to the energy revolution and becoming a forerunner in the green turn, this type of intervention attracts and builds customer loyalty, increasing competitiveness and strengthening corporate image. Finally, contributing now to the green turn will encourage – and accelerate – the development of innovative models and solutions with great benefits for the environment.
