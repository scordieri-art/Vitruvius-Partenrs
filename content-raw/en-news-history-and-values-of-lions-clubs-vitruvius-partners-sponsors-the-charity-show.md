### HISTORY AND VALUES OF LIONS CLUBS: VITRUVIUS & PARTNERS SPONSORS THE CHARITY SHOW

<!-- post-info --> February 17, 2023 · 11:31 am · News

![](https://vitruviusandpartners.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/02/Copertina_Evento_Lions_2023-1024x683.jpg)  
<!-- immagine: immagine in evidenza del post -->

### At the Alfieri Theatre in Asti – yesterday February 16, 2023 – was held the presentation of the show «Chicago 1917. A vision of the future». The show will debut Saturday 25 March at 21 at the Alfieri Theatre in Asti, staged by the Teatro degli Acerbi.

It is the show conceived by Claudio Ligresti, president of the Villanova Lions Club, that brings to the stage the history of the Lions Club through the life of the founder, Melvin Jones.

The dramaturgy is by Patrizia Camatel, also on stage with Fabio Fassio, Federico Gheduzzi, Elena Romano, the vocal coach Alessia Porani. Choreographic movements by Silvia Gatti, sets and costumes by Agnese Falcarin, directed by Fabio Fassio, produced by Vitruvius & Partners and Teatri degli Acerbi. The proceeds of the show will be used to donate a Guide Dog to a blind person.

«The occasion of an evening at the theatre – Ligresti explains – also becomes an addictive mode to make an exemplary story known, that enhances altruism, social commitment, disinterested help, solidarity, and that can offer a point of reflection on the necessity and beauty of acting for the good of others and communities, to help create all together a fairer and more livable world».
