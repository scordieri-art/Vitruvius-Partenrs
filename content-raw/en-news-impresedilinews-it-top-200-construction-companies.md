### IMPRESEDILINEWS.IT: TOP 200 CONSTRUCTION COMPANIES

<!-- post-info --> January 26, 2023 · 8:58 am · News

![](https://vitruviusandpartners.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/01/News_Aziende_200-1024x683.jpg)  
<!-- immagine: immagine in evidenza del post -->

### The 200 construction companies at the top in 2021 show significantly worse income figures than seen for the architecture and engineering companies: in fact if the total turnover (27.1 billion, 31.7 percent in construction sites abroad) increases by 18.9 percent, EBITDA, EBIT and net profit pay decreases of 14.2, 32.7 and 92.3 percent respectively.

The ranking of the top 200 companies in the sector sees the inclusion of 12 companies (general and specialized) whose 2021 financial statements were found in the last month: they are in alphabetical order Aleandri, Bonzi, Costruzioni Italia, D’Agostino Costruzioni Generali, Fast, Ingg. Loy Dona’ Brancaccio – LDB, Irgen Construction, Monaco, Sicea, Sicilsaldo, Strever, Vitruvius & Partners raising the 2021 turnover threshold from 24.5 to 27.2 million.
