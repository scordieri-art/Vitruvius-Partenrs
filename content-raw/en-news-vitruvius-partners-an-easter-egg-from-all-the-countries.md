### VITRUVIUS & PARTNERS: AN EASTER EGG… FROM ALL THE COUNTRIES ‌

<!-- post-info --> March 31, 2024 · 9:00 am · News

![](https://vitruviusandpartners.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/03/pasqua-24--1024x652.jpg)  
<!-- immagine: immagine in evidenza del post -->

As always, the festivities are a much-awaited time for relaxation, but also an opportunity to support the many causes we believe in. This year, for Easter, we have found a way to align with several of them. All equally important.

With the Easter eggs by [Casa Betania’s](https://www.coopaccoglienza.it/cosa-facciamo/case-di-accoglienza/casa-betania/) “Da tutti i paesi” workshop, we are backing a hospitality project which is also a pathway to job inclusion, training, and autonomy for young mothers facing challenges from all corners of the world.

Casa Betania has been around for over 30 years. It is the first facility opened by the social cooperative Accoglienza (in Rome, 1993), and its all-female workshop is a hub for fair trade, arts&crafts, and creative expression.

The tangible value of these activities is crucial, as is the symbolic value of the products that stem from them. They are traces of rebirth, factual and real. For this reason, as well, they seem to be the perfect premise to our well-wishes.

Happy Easter to all from the Vitruvius&Partners team!
