### ON THE OCCASION OF EASTER, WE CHOSE TO SUPPORT CASAMICA BY MAKING A DONATION TO THEIR NON-PROFIT ORGANIZATION!

<!-- post-info --> April 7, 2023 · 8:54 am · News

![](https://vitruviusandpartners.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/04/Happy-Heaster-2023b-1024x683.jpg)  
<!-- immagine: immagine in evidenza del post -->

### CasAmica is a volunteer organization that, since 1986, has been welcoming sick people and their families in need who have to stay away from home, even for long periods, to receive the care they need from hospital facilities.

We thank [CasAmica](https://www.casamica.it/) for the Easter Eggs they sent us in exchange for our contribution.

Happy Easter to all from the Vitruvius team!
