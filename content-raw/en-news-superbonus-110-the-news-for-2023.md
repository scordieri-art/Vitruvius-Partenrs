### After the assumptions, the final rules come to use Superbonus 110% during 2023: from 110% to 90% – here’s who can access it

For 2023 Superbonus 110% is confirmed, with some significant changes: the tax deduction, indeed, will drop to 90%, but the variation will happen gradually. Superbonus 110% will remain valid for condominiums who have deliberated work execution no later than 18 November 2022, with consequent deposit of Cila-S no later than 31 December 2022; or with work execution deliberation of a date between 19 and 24 November 2022 and and deposit of Cilas-s made no later than 25 November 2022.

An extention for Superbonus has been scheduled – to 90% – also for single-family apartments, provided that the building subject to intervention is is the main residence of the taxpayer, who shall have property law and reference income less than 15.000 euros.

In addition, Superbonus 110% will remain so – in the presence of specific requirements – until 31 December 2025 for non-profit organizations and voluntary organizations who carry out health and welfare activities. Instead, non-profit organizations and associations who do not perform the above services can access Superbonus amounting to 90%.

By joining the Superbonus, i twill still be possible to opt for discount in invoice or sale of receivables, for which some innovations have been introduced. In particular, 5 steps will be possible – compared to 4 previously admitted -, of which, following the first free disposal, the additional may be made in favour of qualified entities, such as banks, financial intermediaries and insurance, while the last can be carried out by bank towards account holders VAT numbers.
