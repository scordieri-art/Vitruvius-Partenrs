### VITRUVIUS & PARTNERS SUPPORTS THE “II° INTER-TERRITORIAL TROPHY – MEMORIAL BENEDETTA RAGNI”

<!-- post-info --> June 12, 2023 · 3:59 pm · News

![](https://vitruviusandpartners.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/06/II%C2%B0-TROFEO-INTER-TERRITORIALE-Memorial-Benedetta-Ragni-2-1024x683.jpg)  
<!-- immagine: immagine in evidenza del post -->

### Vitruvius & Partners is pleased to confirm its role as a sponsor and support for the Memorial Benedetta Ragni.

The trophy is a significant Artistic Skating competition for UISP categories. The first two days were held on Saturday, June 10th, and Sunday, June 11th, at the Sports Center in Casteggio, located at Viale Bussolino, n. 7 – 27045 Casteggio. The upcoming dates are scheduled for Saturday, June 17th, and Sunday, June 18th, at the Sports Hall in Stradella, located at Strada Provinciale 201, n. 67 – 27049 Stradella (PV). The event is dedicated to the memory of Benedetta Ragni, a talented athlete from the “Roller Dream Pavia” who lost her life in August 2020 due to pediatric brain cancer.

Admission to the event is free, but any voluntary contributions will be fully donated to the organizing association to support the “[BenedettaPerSempre](https://www.benedettapersempre.com/)“, project, which is part of the ODV “[Bianca Garavaglia](https://www.abianca.org/campagne/benedettapersempre/)” Association, committed to research and treatment of Pediatric Tumors.

Join us at the “II° INTER-TERRITORIAL TROPHY – Memorial Benedetta Ragni” and do your part for the research and treatment of pediatric tumors. Don’t miss this opportunity to support such an important cause.
