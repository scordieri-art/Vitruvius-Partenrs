### VITRUVIUS & PARTNERS ON THE RISE

### Vitruvius & Partners’ 2022 revenue reached 73 million euros, with a growth rate of 109% compared to 2021. Thus the company enters the ranks of big players in the national construction industry, and aims to triple its results by expanding projects and services, both in Italy and abroad.

Milan, May 17th, 2023 – Construction and renovation company, building services provider specialized in requalification and energy efficiency interventions, as well as real estate firm, Vitruvius & Partners confirms its position in the industry with a **2022 revenue of 73 million euros**, more than double compared to the previous year.

The ability to operate on multiple fronts, approached in synergy and with resilience, is a **positive signal for the whole industry**, given its strategic importance and role as an economic driver for the country.

#### The figures in detail

Whereas the **2021 revenue** reached **35 million euros**, securing Vitruvius & Partners the 42nd position in the **ranking of the TOP 60** private construction companies in Italy (source: report Guamari), the **2022 revenu**e reached **73 million** euros, with a **growth rate of 109%**.

Since the start of operations (Jan-2021) over **120 construction projects** **have been completed** across the national territory, mainly distributed among Lombardy, Piedmont, Veneto, Lazio, and Sardinia, with an average duration of 6 months. As for now, there are over **50 active** **construction sites**, which include new projects abroad, starting from Austria.
