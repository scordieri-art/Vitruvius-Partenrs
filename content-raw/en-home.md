# Consolidated experience in the requalification and development of real estate assets, with a focus on energy efficiency.

## Visit the pages dedicated to the main business lines of Vitruvius & Partners group.

<!-- immagine di sfondo (column 11953f8): https://vitruviusandpartners.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/12/Home00007.jpg -->

![](https://vitruviusandpartners.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/12/Vitruvius-Construction-logo-1024x203.png)

Unit of the group specialized in the construction, renovation and building services, with the role of general contractor. By leveraging on internal technical staff as well as proprietary materials and technology, Vitruvius Construction is able to manage every phase of the project, from design to construction, guaranteeing high quality, innovative solutions (with a focus on energy efficiency) and 360° management of the worksite. Given its solidity and structure, Vitruvius Construction is mainly involved in orders of large dimensions and management complexity.

[Visit website](https://vitruvius-construction.com/)  <!-- pulsante -->

<!-- immagine di sfondo (column 7324ea2): https://vitruviusandpartners.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/12/home-page-bg.jpg -->

![](https://vitruviusandpartners.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/12/Vitruvius-Living-Logo.png)

Vitruvius Living is the Real Estate department of the group, specialized in the purchase, resale and management of residential properties. With over 200 properties built annually, Vitruvius Living offers housing solutions that are efficient and harmonious in context, designed to create lasting value and meet the needs of customers attentive to comfort and sustainability.

[Visit website](https://vitruviusliving.com/)  <!-- pulsante -->
