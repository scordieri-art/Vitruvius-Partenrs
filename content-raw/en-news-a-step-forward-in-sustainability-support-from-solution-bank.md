### A STEP FORWARD IN SUSTAINABILITY: SUPPORT FROM SOLUTION BANK

<!-- post-info --> August 18, 2023 · 9:04 am · News

![](https://vitruviusandpartners.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/08/Solution-bank-1024x683.jpg)  
<!-- immagine: immagine in evidenza del post -->

We are excited to share a landmark moment in our growth journey. Vitruvius & Partners recently obtained crucial support from Solution Bank through the award of two strategic loans. These financings, guaranteed by the MCC Guarantee Fund and the EIF – European Investment Fund, InvestEU – Sustainability program, represent a fundamental push towards new perspectives.

#### **Energy Innovation and Market Consolidation**

The opportunity provided by Solution Bank opens the way to new horizons, focused on substantial improvements in energy efficiency and solidification of our market position.

#### **An Even More Promising Future**

We are determined to make the most of this support to pursue our ambitious initiatives: we are building a more sustainable world, one brick at a time.

Together, through this partnership, we are laying the foundation for a better future.
